About this report

This report highlights the X (Twitter) posts from White House-affiliated accounts that have most alienated the X user base, and their verbatim responses. These are the posts with the highest comments-to-like ratio for the selected window, meaning that proportionally more people respond to the post than like it.

Each entry can be expanded to show the original post (embedded from X), and the highest engagement comments on that post. Note that X has a bipartisan user base, and top comments can come from either right- or left-leaning accounts.

How these numbers are counted
  • Ratio is defined as replies per 10 likes.
  • Engagement stats in the post summary are from a snapshot; the expanded version is an embedded post showing current numbers.
  • Top replies are defined as "likes + shares".
  • A post must have at least 500 replies to qualify for this list.
  • All times are in EST.
  • Reposts, reply posts, polls, and direct questions are excluded. A question inside a longer post is kept.
